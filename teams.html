TEAM

Meet the Artisans Behind Zahra

At Zahra, our team is the heart of everything we create. Each member brings expertise, passion, and a deep respect for Middle Eastern culinary heritage. Together, we craft an experience where tradition, creativity, and hospitality come to life.
