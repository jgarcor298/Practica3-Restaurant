Signature Dishes

Lamb Mansaf Royale

The crown jewel of Jordanian cuisine. Tender lamb cooked in fermented dried yogurt sauce, served on a bed of turmeric rice and shrak bread, garnished with toasted almonds and pine nuts.

Zahra Signature Hummus

Ultra-creamy hummus topped with spiced chickpeas, olive oil, and our secret blend of herbs. Served with fresh, warm pita bread straight from our clay oven.

Saffron Grilled Halloumi with Pomegranate Molasses

Golden grilled halloumi cheese drizzled with tangy pomegranate molasses and sprinkled with fresh pomegranate seeds and mint. A perfect balance of savory and sweet.

Chicken Musakhan Roll

Sumac-spiced chicken with caramelized onions, rolled in thin shrak bread and baked to crisp perfection. Served with a side of garlic yogurt dip.

Kunafa with Orange Blossom Syrup

Crispy spun pastry layered with gooey sweet cheese, soaked in aromatic orange blossom syrup and garnished with crushed pistachios.
